# Anki Quick Add – feature tour, ~2 min, 16:9 (YouTube)

Preamble: English narration (edge-tts en-US-AndrewNeural). Every screenshot in the frames is a real
capture from `scripts/capture.ts` – the cards were generated on that run by Gemini 3.5 Flash and added
to a real Anki through AnkiConnect; nothing is mocked. Numbers in the narration come from the code
(12 UI languages, ~40 language pairs, 4 provider families). Not shown: the native right-click menu
(cannot be captured), other people's products.

---

## Segment 01 – Hook (0:00–0:08)

**Екран:** 01a-hook: dark stage, extension icon, headline "One word in. A complete Anki card out.",
subline with the pipeline (translation · IPA · examples · audio · image). 01b-card: the finished
"lighthouse" card (back side) as Anki renders it.

> Meet Anki Quick Add. Type a word, and a complete flashcard lands in Anki – translation, pronunciation, examples, audio, and a picture.

## Segment 02 – Popup (0:08–0:24)

**Екран:** 02a-popup-empty: the popup right after Alt+A. 02b-popup-typing: "lighthouse" typed, the
instant translation under the field. 02c-popup-adding: Enter pressed, "Adding…". 02d-popup-added:
"Added: lighthouse – маяк", the word in the history line.

> Open it with Alt plus A. As you type, an instant translation appears. Press Enter, and the extension asks your language model for the translation, transcription, part of speech, examples and grammar notes, fetches pronunciation audio and a Wikipedia image, and adds the note through AnkiConnect.

## Segment 03 – The card (0:24–0:32)

**Екран:** 03a-card-front: front side (word, IPA, audio icon). 03b-card-back: back side with the
translation, the lighthouse photo, three examples, synonyms, grammar note and the image credit.

> Here is the card it just made. Real templates, real media, attribution included.

## Segment 04 – Selection bubble and context (0:32–0:48)

**Екран:** 04a-bubble: Wikipedia "Baseball", the word "bat" selected, the bubble with the instant
translation "кажан" (the animal). 04b-bubble-added: after one click – "Added: bat – битка, біта, палиця"
(the baseball bat). Caption: "the sentence travels with the word".

> Reading something? Hold Shift, select a word, and a bubble shows an instant translation. One click adds the card. And because the sentence goes along as context, "bat" on a baseball page becomes the baseball bat, not the animal.

## Segment 05 – Editor (0:48–0:56)

**Екран:** 05a-editor: the editor window for "serendipity" – translations, IPA, examples, grammar,
audio player, the Wikipedia portrait, deck selector, "Regenerate" with a hint field.

> Want to check first? Shift plus Enter, or Edit in the bubble, opens an editor: change any field, drop the image, or regenerate with a hint.

## Segment 06 – Providers and languages (0:56–1:08)

**Екран:** 06a-providers: Settings → Providers (provider, key, model list, effort).
06b-generation: Settings → Languages & Generation (source/target, CEFR level, counts, toggles, media).

> Bring your own key: OpenAI, Google, Anthropic, or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint, even a local model. Pick any of forty languages, the CEFR level of the examples, and exactly which parts to generate.

## Segment 07 – Note types and preview (1:08–1:17)

**Екран:** 07a-mapping: Settings → Anki with the field-mapping table. 07b-preview: the preview tab
rendering the note type's real templates.

> Use the built-in note type, or map the generated parts onto your own, and preview the result with your real Anki templates.

## Segment 08 – Language and theme (1:17–1:27)

**Екран:** 08a-general: Settings → General, "Interface language" and "Interface theme" selects, the
theme set to "Dark on a schedule" with the two time fields. 08b-general-dark: the same tab with the
dark theme forced.

> The interface speaks twelve languages: follow the browser, or pick one in the settings. And it comes in light, dark, or dark on a schedule, for late-night sessions.

## Segment 09 – Batch (1:27–1:35)

**Екран:** 09a-batch-list: four words pasted into List mode. 09b-batch-running: the first one added,
the next one running. 09c-batch-done: "4 added · 0 duplicates · 0 errors".

> Have a list? Paste it. Words are added one by one, and duplicates are skipped or updated, your call.

## Segment 10 – CTA (1:35–1:43)

**Екран:** 10a-cta: icon, "Anki Quick Add", three lines (Free & open source · Your keys, your Anki ·
12 UI languages), GitHub URL and "Chrome Web Store".

> Anki Quick Add is free and open source. Your keys, your Anki, nothing in between. Get it from the Chrome Web Store, or grab it on GitHub.
