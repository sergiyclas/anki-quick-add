### Description (*copy*)

Anki Quick Add is a Chrome extension that turns a single word into a finished Anki flashcard:
translation, IPA, part of speech, example sentences, synonyms, a grammar note, pronunciation audio
and a Wikipedia image with its license. It works with no API key at all, or with the LLM provider
and key you choose. The note goes straight into your own Anki through AnkiConnect.

Everything in this video is a real capture of the extension running against a real Anki collection –
the cards you see were generated during the recording, nothing is mocked up.

Get it:
• GitHub (source, MIT): https://github.com/sergiyclas/anki-quick-add
• Chrome Web Store: (link once the listing is live)
• AnkiConnect add-on (required): https://ankiweb.net/shared/info/2055492159

What it does
• Free by default: translations, part of speech and definitions from Google Translate's dictionary
  data, IPA and examples from dictionaryapi.dev, example sentences from the Tatoeba corpus.
• Or your own key: OpenAI, Google Gemini, Anthropic, or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint, including a
  local model in Ollama or LM Studio. Keys stay in your browser.
• Around 40 languages in any pair, examples at the CEFR level you pick.
• Built-in note type, or a field mapping onto a note type you already use, with a preview that
  renders your real Anki templates.
• Selection bubble, right-click menu, editor window, list mode, duplicate policy.
• Interface in 12 languages, light / dark / scheduled dark theme.

No account, no telemetry, no backend. Images come from Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons and keep
their author and license on the card. The narration is generated speech.

## 5. Regenerating the images

```bash
node scripts/youtube-assets.mjs   # banner, avatar, watermark, thumbnail
```

The thumbnail embeds `video/raw/card-02-back.png`, so re-run `npx tsx scripts/capture.ts` first if
the card design changes.
